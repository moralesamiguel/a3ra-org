A3RA

Version: v1.0

Accountable Action for Autonomous and Cyber-Physical Systems.

Canonical: This site represents the canonical formulation of A3RA.

Principle

Trustworthy actions must be:

  • Attributable
  • Admissible
  • Auditable
  • Recoverable
  • Within an explicit Authority Scope
