Principle

Trustworthy actions must be:

  • Attributable
  • Admissible
  • Auditable
  • Recoverable
  • Within an explicit Authority Scope

Scope

A3RA defines a mission for trustworthy action. It does not certify products, prescribe vendors, or claim regulatory authority. The goal is to make accountable action in cyber-physical systems legible, implementable, and economically viable.
